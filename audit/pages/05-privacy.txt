=== LOGIC ISSUES ===
1. The "Back" button uses navigate(-1) which goes to the browser's previous history entry. If a user navigates directly to /privacy from a bookmark or external link, navigate(-1) would take them out of the app entirely (to whatever was in their browser history before). A "← Home" or "← Back to Home" link would be safer.
2. The contact email (privacy@agentin.chat) is rendered as a styled `<span>` not a `<a href="mailto:...">` link. On mobile, users cannot tap to compose an email. This is a usability issue.
3. No document.title update — the browser tab will show whatever the default app title is (likely "Agentin" or "GeekSpace") rather than "Privacy Policy | Agentin". This is bad for accessibility (screen readers) and UX.
4. The "Last updated: February 2026" date is hardcoded. It will become stale if the policy is updated without updating this string.
5. Content depth is minimal — covers the 5 key topics but lacks:
   - Cookies policy section
   - GDPR-specific rights (right to access, right to erasure, data portability — though deletion IS mentioned)
   - CCPA compliance section (if targeting US users)
   - Contact for DPA (Data Processing Agreement) for enterprise users
   - Any mention of analytics tools used (if any)
6. The "SOC 2 Compliant" and "GDPR Ready" badges shown on the landing page are not backed by or linked from this Privacy Policy page.
7. The trust badges claim "GDPR Ready" on the landing page but the privacy policy doesn't mention GDPR explicitly.
