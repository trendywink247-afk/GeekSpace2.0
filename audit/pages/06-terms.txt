=== LOGIC ISSUES ===
1. Same navigate(-1) issue as PrivacyPage — navigates out of app if arrived via direct link/bookmark.
2. Same contact email span issue — legal@agentin.chat is not a tappable link.
3. Same missing document.title update.
4. Same hardcoded "Last updated: February 2026" date.

Content-specific concerns:
5. "Credits & Billing" section describes a credits-based system with a 14-day refund policy. However, there is currently no visible billing/payment UI in the publicly audited pages. If credits are managed in the dashboard but not yet purchasable, the ToS billing terms are premature and could create legal ambiguity if challenged.
6. "Limitation of Liability" is minimal — for a production service with user data, automations running on behalf of users, and AI-generated outputs, this clause may be legally insufficient without jurisdiction specification, governing law clause, or dispute resolution process.
7. No governing law or jurisdiction clause — where do disputes get resolved?
8. No age requirement mentioned (e.g., "must be 13+" for COPPA compliance or "must be 18+" for full contractual capacity).
9. No modification policy — how will users be notified when terms change? The current terms are silent on this.
10. "Acceptable Use" forbids spam, harassment, rate limit circumvention — but does not clarify what "AI agent" actions count as user responsibility (the "Limitation of Liability" says users are responsible for reviewing outputs, but the Acceptable Use doesn't explicitly say users are responsible for what their agent does on their behalf).
11. The Credits & Billing section implies a commercial product ("14-day refund policy for unused credits"). The current signup flow uses free credits (15000 credits on signup per auth.ts). If the platform is invite-only or free during beta, these billing terms may confuse users.
